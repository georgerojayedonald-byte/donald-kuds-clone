ANY QUESTIONS?

What products does Kuda offer?

Kuda offers access to affordable and rewarding banking products including credit like overdrafts and loans, investments in US stocks, physical and virtual cards, Premium accounts with exclusive perks, a range of savings products, and 25 free transfers to any bank, every month.

What services does Kuda offer?

Kuda allows you to make payments for essentials and lifestyle items from right inside the app including airtime, betting, cardless payments, electricity, gift cards, internet, transport, and TV.

How is Kuda different from other banks?

We’re digital-first, so almost everything you’d queue up for at a traditional bank happens right on your phone. Open an account, send money, pay bills, freeze your card, or get help, all on the Kuda app. We’ll even deliver your debit card to any address in Nigeria.

We also won’t charge you unnecessary fees for banking with us and if you’d rather talk to someone in person, you can visit our branch at 1-11 Commercial Avenue, Yaba, Lagos, Nigeria.
